Where a human (me) had to step in: I approved every payment myself on the dashboard. The Boss as the convener

In this run there wasn't a role that played the convener, someone who pulls everyone together so the team works around the table instead of passing each ticket down its own flowchart. Maybe it's not about adding a new role for a convener. Maybe the Boss could play the convener role, so we could prioritize these things in one session instead of trying to solve them one by one.

Why I think so: that's essentially what allowed me to resolve all three tickets. I had enough information to know that I should resolve ticket 103 first, before trying to resolve the other tickets. When 103 ran first, the team ranked the bills, filed rent and invoice 501, and filled the hoodie order from stock. After that, 101 and 102 only had to confirm. In my first run (Nov 2), the tickets went one by one in a different order, and 103 never "knew" that rent was competing for the same cash. A Boss acting as convener would read the whole board first (all tickets, all bills, all stock) and set that order itself.
